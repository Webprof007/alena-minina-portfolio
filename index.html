About the Project

Pas Fou is a carbon footprint assessment platform that helps users calculate and understand their environmental impact and identify actions they can take to reduce their emissions.

The core of the product is a complex dynamic questionnaire covering different areas of everyday life.

Unlike a traditional linear survey, the questionnaire adapts its structure to each user. Answers can dynamically create individual entities — such as vehicles or household members — which then become part of subsequent question flows.

Questions can be repeated, adapted or conditionally displayed for each specific entity.

Users can calculate not only their own footprint but also work with household members and provide answers on their behalf.

After completing the assessment, users can choose actions they are willing to take to reduce their emissions and track the resulting reduction impact.

My Role

  • Project Management
  • Product Logic
  • QA

My Contribution

Worked on product logic, requirements, development coordination and testing of a complex questionnaire-driven system.

A key part of the product was the dynamic entity logic.

Example: A user can specify that they own three vehicles. The system creates three separate vehicle entities. The user can name each vehicle. Later questions related to vehicles are generated and answered separately for each specific vehicle.

The same principle applies to household members and other dependent scenarios.
